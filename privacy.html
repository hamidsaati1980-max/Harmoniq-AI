Privacy Policy – Harmoniq AI

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Harmoniq AI respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when you use Harmoniq AI.

1. Information We Collect

Harmoniq AI does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the application.

The application may store preferences locally on your device, such as favorite songs, playlists, recently played tracks, language settings, and application preferences.

2. Music and Third-Party Services

Harmoniq AI may use third-party music services to search for and play available music. Search requests may be sent to the relevant third-party service.

3. Advertising

Harmoniq AI may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect or use advertising identifiers, device information, and usage information according to applicable laws and user settings.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Harmoniq AI itself does not use cookies to personally identify users. Third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Harmoniq AI is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Services

Harmoniq AI may use third-party music and advertising services. Their privacy practices are governed by their respective privacy policies.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Harmoniq AI, you can contact the application developer through the official Harmoniq AI project page.
